# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful impact.
- Welcome questions and differing technical perspectives.
- Protect users' privacy and financial information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and improve.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Deliberate disruption, intimidation, or sustained bad-faith participation.
- Misusing access to repository, broker, or user data.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. The maintainer may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors based on the nature and history of the behavior. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
